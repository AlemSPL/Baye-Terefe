About me

Geospatial science for environmental understanding and evidence-based decision support.

I hold an MSc in GIS and Remote Sensing from Mekelle University and a BA in Geography and Environmental Studies from Mizan Tepi University. I have served as a lecturer at Injibara University since September 2019. Since 2025, I have been pursuing my PhD at the Faculty of Geographical Science and Engineering, Henan University, China.

My research connects Earth-observation data, spatial analysis, and computational modelling to investigate environmental dynamics and support evidence-based planning. I am particularly interested in reproducible geospatial workflows and integrating remote sensing with machine learning.

Research directions

Questions, methods, and applications

Research themes represented in my teaching, publications, and geospatial work.

01 / EARTH OBSERVATION

Remote sensing and environmental change

Satellite time series, land-cover dynamics, vegetation condition, land-surface temperature, drought monitoring, and landscape assessment.

02 / COMPUTATIONAL METHODS

GeoAI and spatial data science

Machine learning, geospatial feature engineering, spatial prediction, explainable modelling, and reproducible Python-based workflows.

03 / ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS

Ecosystem services and natural resources

Spatial assessment of ecosystem services, soil erosion, watershed conservation, water resources, and agricultural suitability.

04 / CLOUD-NATIVE GEOSPATIAL

Cloud-native GIS and remote sensing

Scalable, reproducible geospatial workflows using Python, STAC-based data discovery, cloud-optimized data formats, and distributed processing for Earth-observation analysis.

05 / GEOSPATIAL BIG DATA

Big-data analytics for Earth observation

Efficient processing and integration of large satellite time series and environmental datasets to support spatiotemporal analysis, modelling, and evidence-based decision support.

06 / RISK & RESILIENCE

Climate variability and disaster risk

Geospatial approaches to climate-risk assessment, drought dynamics, environmental vulnerability, and decision support.
